--- DESCRIPCIÓN COMPLETA ---
Moments is a private social network where every moment stays yours.

Share your day with the people you choose—without feeling like everything has to be perfect, public, or made to please everyone. Photos, videos, stories, messages, places, memories, and the little things that only make sense with your friends.

Moments is built to feel closer: less noise, more intention, and more control over what you share.

HIDDEN LAYERS
Some posts hide something more.

With Hidden Layers you can add text, audio, or images inside a photo. Friends discover them by tapping the moment, and you can schedule them to unlock later.

A secret note. A voice. A clue. An image inside another. Something you don’t see at first—but it was there.

STORIES WITH MORE MAGIC
Create stories with stickers to express yourself, play, and tell what’s happening better. Including:

Reveal: scratch to uncover hidden content.
Polaroid: add an image with style and a caption.
Audio: record voice or ambient sound right in your story.

PRIVACY WITHOUT THE HASSLE
You decide who sees each moment:

best friends
custom audiences
only me
private lists
comment control
hide your followers and following
hide content from specific people
block or mute accounts and words

CHAT AND REAL CONNECTIONS
Talk with friends through private encrypted messages, photos, videos, audio, and ephemeral content. Share when you want, with who you want.

NOVA, YOUR AI ASSISTANT
Nova helps you inside Moments—remembering preferences, understanding context, and offering useful suggestions without taking control away. Conversations with Nova are designed to feel personal and protected.

Moments isn’t about posting more.
It’s about sharing better.

Get it on Google Play and share only with the people who truly matter.
